Does purchase history help?

In this experiment, it did not. Adding history slightly worsened predictions on held-out households. This is a small decision-support prototype using fictional data, not a validated forecast of US shoppers.

What to test next

I do not know the best launch combination yet. Run 4’s models mostly rank $3.49 / environment / online highest for purchase probability, but one training fold prefers cleaning. These are associations, and there are no margin data.

A bounded next test: one randomized online study with real US shoppers, comparing $3.49 vs $4.49 and cleaning vs an independently substantiated environmental message. Keep the formula, bottle, competing offers and audience constant; include “buy none.” Measure actual purchase and follow up for repeat purchase. Online is a practical test venue, not a proven superior channel.

Predefine the minimum worthwhile profit improvement after costs and power the study for it. Change the choice if the price discount fails to pay for itself, cleaning outperforms the environmental message, or first purchases do not translate into repeat purchases. A retail/channel comparison remains untested.

Fictional households and unverified product claims. Model score contributions explain the calculation; they do not establish a shopper’s motivation. No API, GPU, or external model service is used.
