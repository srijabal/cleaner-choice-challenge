After the first comparison I thought maybe the model was fitting noise because it had too much freedom. So I tested stronger regularization, which keeps the learned effects smaller. Without history, log loss improved from 1.5909 to 1.5731. With history it improved from 1.5952 to 1.5706. So this helped both models. But the small history advantage was still uncertain, so I could not say history really helped. My next question was whether the inputs added anything beyond predicting average choice rates. These were still comparisons on reused build data, so I would not call them fresh confirmation.
